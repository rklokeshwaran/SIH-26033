FARMAR'S FRIEND

## Problem Description
• What is the problem?
    Many Farmers are facing the problem of low prices for the crops they cultivated. Mostly intermediates earn lot through the crops. If there is the high demand they try to buy the crops and vegetables with price the they sell it with high price to the consumers.

## Proposed Solution

**Farmer's Friend** is an AI-integrated web platform where farmers list crops and vegetables at their declared prices while consumers can bargain directly on the platform. Produce undergoes quality examination before sale and is delivered within **1 to 2 days**. It also recommends crops that can be grown without artificial fertilizers based on local farm weather and climate, connecting farmers and consumers directly with zero middlemen or platform fees to improve produce freshness, health outcomes, and overall Human Development Index (HDI).

## Technical Approach

- **Technologies:** HTML and CSS for the frontend, Django for the backend, SQL for database management, and an AI/ML module trained on crop, climate, and yield data.
- **Methodology & Implementation:**
  1. **Research & Design:** Map direct farmer-to-consumer workflows, bargaining rules, pre-sale quality check stages, and crop-climate recommendation logic.
  2. **Development:** Build the Django web application, SQL data models, bargaining/order flow, and the AI/ML crop recommendation engine.
  3. **Testing:** Validate price negotiation, order/delivery tracking (**1–2 days**), quality check workflows, and weather/climate crop suggestions.
  4. **Deployment / Demo:** Host a working prototype and demonstrate end-to-end farmer listing, bargaining, and AI crop recommendations.

## Feasibility and Viability

- **Feasibility:** Built on a web-first architecture using HTML/CSS, Django, and SQL, making it practical to prototype and deploy with standard web hosting, structured agricultural/weather datasets, and straightforward order/delivery coordination.
- **Challenges & Risks:** Perishable produce quality/spoilage before **1–2 day** delivery, price disputes during bargaining, reluctance to skip artificial fertilizers initially, and coordinating reliable delivery logistics.
- **Mitigation Strategies:** Enforce pre-sale quality checks, set clear bargaining bounds so farmers retain fair value, provide climate-matched low-input crop recommendations, and batch local deliveries within the **1–2 day** window.

## Impact and Benefits

- **Target Audience Impact:** Farmers set their own prices, bargain directly without middlemen cuts, and get AI-guided crop choices suited to local weather; consumers get fresher produce, direct bargaining, and **1–2 day** delivery.
- **Broader Benefits:**
  - *Economic:* Higher earnings for farmers and fairer prices for consumers with zero middlemen or platform fees.
  - *Social & Health:* Direct farmer-consumer access and fresher produce support better nutrition and contribute to higher HDI.
  - *Environmental:* Recommending crops suited to local climate without artificial fertilizers reduces chemical runoff and supports sustainable cultivation.
